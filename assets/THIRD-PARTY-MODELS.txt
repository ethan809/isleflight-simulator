ISLEFLIGHT — AIRCRAFT AND CAR MODEL ATTRIBUTION
======================================

Meshes in this website are authored by independent artists and have been
converted, reoriented, scaled, and/or recolored for Isleflight. The names of
aircraft manufacturers and airlines do not imply endorsement.

B737.flightgear.glb (the new default Boeing 737 exterior)
  Original: FlightGear 737-800 exterior by the FGMEMBERS/737-800 contributors
  Source: https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/737-800
  License: GNU General Public License version 2 (GPL-2.0)
  This is an actual FlightGear aircraft, not geometry designed for Isleflight.
  The original AC3D fuselage, left/right wing, stabilizers, winglets and nose
  gear are preserved as B737.flightgear.source.ac, LWing.ac, RWing.ac,
  HorzStab.ac, VertStab.ac, winglet.ac, and nosegear.ac; the original
  737-800.png texture is also provided. The edited conversion script is
  Concorde.converter.py (supports both aircraft), and a copy of GPL-2.0
  is included in LICENSE-GPL-2.0.txt.
